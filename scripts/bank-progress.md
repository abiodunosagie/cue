# Question bank progress

Target: about 300 technical Q&A (src/bank/qa) and 200 coding problems (src/bank/coding).
Every coding item must pass `node scripts/verify-bank.js`. `npm test` checks shape and matching.

Rules for every item:
- Q&A: first person, spoken, calm senior voice, 60 to 260 words, no long dashes, no invented
  stories or numbers, never volunteer a technology the question did not ask about, no tool or
  product names unless the question is about them, 4 to 6 natural phrasings (casual ones too).
- Coding: statement in our own words (never copied), LeetCode-style signatures, Dart and
  TypeScript, 3 to 6 //@step marks matching the steps list, tests with edge cases.
